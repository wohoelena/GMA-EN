Policy

Community Guidelines

Guidelines for keeping community communication educational, respectful, and within scope.

Respect

Keep discussion constructive

Use respectful language, stay on topic, and support a learning environment for participants at different knowledge levels.

Scope

Keep questions educational

Frame questions around concepts, study process, terminology, and course materials rather than personal decisions or product choices.

Safety

Do not share sensitive information

Avoid posting personal financial details, account information, identity documents, private credentials, or confidential third-party material.

Moderation

Moderation may apply

Content may be removed or redirected if it falls outside the education scope or creates legal, privacy, conduct, or safety concerns.
