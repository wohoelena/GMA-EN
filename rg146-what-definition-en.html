RG146 Definition

RG146-related study is a reference framework in finance education learning. It helps explain the knowledge, skills, and communication boundaries that learners should understand in relevant contexts.

CORE CONCEPT

Understand what it is and what it is not

01

Reference framework

A learning reference used to explain relevant knowledge, skills, and communication boundaries.

02

Knowledge framework

Focuses on product knowledge, risk awareness, communication scope, and service boundaries.

03

Not personal permission

Study itself does not grant workplace permission or professional service authority.

04

Study output

Learners should be able to explain the framework in careful, outcome-neutral language.

One

RG146 as a training-standard framework

RG146-related learning can be used as a framework for understanding finance education knowledge and communication boundaries. Its role is to help learners understand product knowledge, risk awareness, compliance-style language, and client-communication boundaries.

Understanding the definition before studying helps learners distinguish objectives: which topics are product knowledge, which are professional language, and which require further confirmation through role arrangements, disclosure duties, and service scope.

Two

From product knowledge to communication boundaries

  • Basic financial product knowledge.
  • Educational understanding of risk and return features.
  • The difference between general information, general advice, personal advice, and service boundaries.
  • Disclosure-style documents, client-communication boundaries, and record requirements.
  • Institution, role, and responsibility relationships.

The shared goal is to help learners describe the finance education framework in careful language rather than treating course content as product recommendation.

Three

Do not equate learning results with professional permission

Different roles, product ranges, and service activities may involve different learning and confirmation requirements. Learners should not treat general course content as a direct conclusion about role suitability.

This page is for general education and professional-literacy building. It does not mean that completing learning grants permission to provide financial services. Role requirements should be confirmed through employers, education providers, professional organisations, or appropriately qualified professionals.

Four

What learners should be able to explain

After this module, learners should be able to describe RG146-related study as a professional learning framework rather than a personal permission or career result. A suitable explanation should mention product knowledge, risk language, communication boundaries, role confirmation, and the need to check current requirements through the appropriate channel.

This wording keeps the learning purpose clear and avoids turning educational content into a statement about individual suitability.
