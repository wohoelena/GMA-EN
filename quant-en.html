Modules

Three course pathways

Each pathway builds a different layer of research capability: concept clarity, process discipline, and Python-supported analysis.

Module 1

Quantitative Research Foundations

Understand data, rules, sample analysis, and basic risk-awareness language.

Module 2

Strategy Automation Training

Prepare the research environment, define rules, run historical checks, and manage review workflows.

Module 3

Python Quantitative Research

Use Python for data preparation, signal records, sample testing, and AI-assisted research review.

Learning Journey

A responsible sequence for new learners

Stage 1

Learn the language

Start with research concepts, rule documentation, evidence limits, and risk-awareness terminology.

Stage 2

Define the process

Move into environment setup, rule definition, historical review, and observation workflow management.

Stage 3

Use Python carefully

Apply Python to clean data, record signals, summarize samples, and document AI-assisted checks.

Stage 4

Review conservatively

Separate educational observations from personal conclusions, and keep assumptions visible in every summary.

Boundaries

How this learning area stays conservative

General education

No personal advice

Content is designed to explain research workflows and terminology, not to provide individualized financial, legal, or tax guidance.

No recommendations

No product direction

Examples discuss data handling and sample interpretation without directing learners toward any specific financial product or action.

No promised outcomes

Evidence has limits

Historical samples are treated as study material. They do not remove uncertainty or imply future learning, career, or market outcomes.
